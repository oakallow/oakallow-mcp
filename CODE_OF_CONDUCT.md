# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want taking part in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

1. Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
2. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
3. Give feedback about the work, not the person.
4. Respect people's privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information, account details,
   or credentials without their explicit permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

1. Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
2. Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
3. Discriminatory jokes or language.
4. Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
5. Posting security vulnerabilities publicly instead of reporting them through
   [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
6. Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
   setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and
discussions, and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **hello@oakallow.io**. Reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially.

The maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently block
anyone whose behavior is harmful.
